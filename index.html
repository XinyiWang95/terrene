Our Story

Every bracelet begins with a seed.

Not a factory mold. Not a synthetic bead. A real seed. A coffee bean. A fruit stone.

We believe what you wear should have roots. Terrene means "of the earth" — and that's exactly where our jewelry comes from. Each piece is handcrafted from nature's own treasures: aromatic coffee beans, smooth tagua nuts, delicate acai seeds.

The best stories don't start in factories. They start in the soil.

What We Work With

Every material is chosen by nature, refined by hand.

☕

Coffee Beans

Sourced from small farms. Each bean carries its origin's aroma and story — slowly dried, carefully drilled, hand-strung.

🫘

Tagua Nuts

Known as "vegetable ivory" — smooth, durable, and elephant-friendly. A sustainable alternative that feels like silk.

🪵

Acai & Lotus Seeds

Tiny treasures from the Amazon and beyond. Each seed is naturally unique in shape, color, and texture.

🪨

Natural Stones

Sandalwood, lava rock, coconut shell — we pair botanicals with earth's minerals for depth and balance.
